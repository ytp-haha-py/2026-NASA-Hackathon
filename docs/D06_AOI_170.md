# D06 Moon/Mars regolith AOI evidence (#170 Goal F)

**Scope:** bounded, AOI-scoped D06 regolith evidence at one Moon measurement site
and one Mars measurement site. This is **not a global or exhaustive survey** and
does not change the shared D06 coverage matrix, scoring, API schemas or existing
D06 bundles.

**Artifacts:** `pipelines/d05_d06_aoi/regolith/` (portable typed-v2 release,
source tables, builder, QA, manifest, report). **Tests:**
`pipelines/tests/test_d06_aoi_170.py`. **Builder:**
`python3 pipelines/d05_d06_aoi/regolith/build_release.py --check`.

The older `docs/D05_D06_AOI_170.md` bundle used the Shackleton rim and Jezero
CTX windows, where no D06 regolith measurement exists. This release adds the
measurement-site AOIs needed to return actual D06 values, while keeping every
missing property as explicit `no_coverage`.

## 1. AOIs

Both AOIs use planetary body-fixed/geographic frames, never Earth WGS84.

| area_id | body | site | bbox (planetary CRS) | CRS | support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `moon-apollo15-station8-regolith-170` | Moon | Apollo 15 Station 8 / LSRP trench, Hadley-Apennine | W 3.6293, S 26.1336, E 3.6303, N 26.1346 | `IAU_2015:30100` | ~100 m site envelope; ALSEP centre 26.1341 N, 3.6298 E |
| `mars-insight-hp3-regolith-170` | Mars | InSight HP3/mole site, Elysium Planitia | W 135.6225, S 4.5015, E 135.6235, N 4.5025 | `IAU_2015:49900` | ~100 m site envelope; lander 4.502 N, 135.623 E |

The values are site/sample-specific. A bbox is a locator envelope, not an area
average and not a claim that the property is uniform across the bbox.

## 2. Returned values and explicit no-coverage

### Moon — Apollo 15 Station 8

Source: NASA SP-289, Chapter 7 *Soil-Mechanics Experiment* (PDS bundle
`urn:nasa:pds:a15lsrp_soil_mechanics`, version 1.0, DOI `10.17189/vbbq-sd20`).
Table 7-IV gives the Station 8 SRP-derived estimates for the upper 10–20 cm.
The PSR summary gives the Station 8 trench cohesion.

| feature | state | value | unit | depth | evidence class | source locator |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| `bulk_density_kg_m3` | detected | 1970 | kg/m^3 | 0–0.20 m | derived from in-situ penetrometer (SRP) | PSR Table 7-IV: 1.97 g/cm3 (1.92–2.01) |
| `porosity_fraction` | detected | 0.365 | fraction | 0–0.20 m | derived from SRP/porosity correlation | PSR Table 7-IV: 36.5% (35–38%) |
| `friction_angle_deg` | detected | 49.5 | degree | 0–0.20 m | derived from porosity–friction correlation | PSR Table 7-IV: 49.5° (47.5–51.5) |
| `cohesion_kpa` | detected | 1.0 | kPa | 0–0.36 m (trench exposure) | derived from trench-wedge failure | PSR summary: ~1 kN/m2; compilation row records 0.94 kPa |
| `particle_size_distribution` | `no_coverage` | — | fraction | unknown | — | PSR grain-size curves are figures, not a typed bin table |
| `bearing_strength_kpa` | `no_coverage` | — | kPa | unknown | — | PSR Table 7-III has penetration resistance, not bearing strength |

**Key caveats.** The 1970 kg/m3, 0.365 and 49.5° values are **published
estimates/derivations**, not direct mass/volume or shear measurements. The PSR
assumes specific gravity Gs = 3.1; the PSR also notes a direct drill-core density
of 1.84 g/cm3 from the same area, lower than the SRP estimate. The discrepancy is
preserved as a limitation, not averaged away. The 1.0 kPa cohesion is a
trench-scale back-analysis with preliminary end-effect uncertainty; it is not a
direct shear test. No PSD and no bearing strength were ingested.

### Mars — InSight HP3/mole site

Source: Delage et al. (2024), *Investigating the Martian soil at the InSight
landing site*, Soils and Rocks 47(3):e2024005023, DOI
`10.28927/SR.2024.005023`, CC BY 4.0. HP3 bundle
`urn:nasa:pds:insight_hp3_tem` (version 1.0, DOI `10.17189/1517573`) supplies the
underlying thermal measurements.

| feature | state | value | unit | depth | evidence class | source locator |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| `bulk_density_kg_m3` | detected | 1491 | kg/m^3 | 0–0.40 m | model-derived from HP3 thermal-conductivity inversion | Delage §5.2.2: 1491 +121/−113 kg/m3; **no direct density measurement** |
| `cohesion_kpa` | detected | 5.8 | kPa | 0–0.07 m (mole pit) | derived from in-situ scoop tip push + slope stability | Delage §5.2.3: minimum c′ = 5.8 kPa; φ′ fixed at 30° |
| `particle_size_distribution` | `no_coverage` | — | fraction | unknown | — | literature grain-size scalar ~104–173 µm is not a distribution |
| `porosity_fraction` | `no_coverage` | — | fraction | unknown | — | no direct porosity; no density→porosity conversion without particle density/model |
| `friction_angle_deg` | `no_coverage` | — | degree | unknown | — | 30° is an assumed model input, not a measurement |
| `bearing_strength_kpa` | `no_coverage` | — | kPa | unknown | — | 5.3 MPa mole penetration resistance is not bearing strength |

**Key caveats.** The density is explicitly **model-derived**; the paper states
there are no direct density measurements at the site. The cohesion is a
**minimum inferred** from a scoop-push slope-stability back-analysis with
friction angle fixed at 30°, not a direct shear test. The 0.4 kPa flat-push
minimum is not averaged with the 5.8 kPa tip-push value. Penetration resistance
is not relabeled as bearing strength.

## 4. Cross-checks and audits

- **Moon PSR vs compilation:** PSR Table 7-IV (1.97 g/cm3, 36.5%, 49.5°,
  0–20 cm) matches Gasteiner CSV row 100 (1.97, 35–38%, 47.5–51.5°, 0–20 cm).
- **Moon cohesion:** PSR summary ~1 kN/m2; Gasteiner row 101 records 0.94 kPa for
  the same trench experiment. The release keeps the published range 0.94–1.0 kPa.
- **Moon direct-core discrepancy:** PSR notes a direct drill-core density of
  1.84 g/cm3 from the same area, lower than the SRP-derived 1.97 g/cm3. This is
  recorded as a documented discrepancy, not silently reconciled.
- **Mars density:** Delage et al. explicitly state no direct density measurement;
  the 1491 kg/m3 value is a thermal-conductivity inversion and is flagged
  `density_not_directly_measured`.
- **Mars friction:** the 30° value is a fixed model input, so
  `friction_angle_deg` is `no_coverage`.
- **Mars bearing:** the 5.3 MPa mole penetration resistance is not bearing
  strength, so `bearing_strength_kpa` is `no_coverage`.

### Soil chemistry (audit-only)

Real Mars APXS bulk chemistry exists at the Gobabeb scoop target (sol 1225):
Na2O 2.35, MgO 9.32, Al2O3 9.16, SiO2 45.74, SO3 2.87, Cl 0.49, K2O 0.39,
CaO 7.13, TiO2 0.84, FeO 19.97 wt% (with per-oxide errors). It is recorded in
`audit.json` as `unsupported_by_registry`, because D06 `FEATURES_V2` has no
soil-chemistry feature. It is not used to infer any physical property. Moon soil
chemistry is likewise not ingested; no registered D06 chemistry feature exists.

### Thermal inertia (audit-only, D03)

The existing D03 Mars THEMIS quantitative thermal-inertia product
(`THEMIS_TI_Mosaic_Quant_00N060E_100mpp`, edition 1, 100 m, DOI
`10.1029/2006JE002735`) is recorded in `audit.json`. It is **not** converted to
D06 bulk density: D06 semantics forbids thermal inertia → density without a
verified physical model and stated conductivity/heat-capacity assumptions. No
conversion is applied here.

### Regolith thickness (unsupported)

`regolith_thickness_m` is not registered in the current D06 `FEATURES_V2`
registry. Apollo 15 trench/drill depths and InSight mole/duricrust depths are
local sampling/profile depths, not a regolith-thickness product. A shared
registry/contract change would be required (#141); it is not made here.

## 5. Forbidden derivations (explicitly not performed)

- DEM roughness/slope → grain size, cohesion, friction angle or bearing strength.
- Land-cover bare fraction → regolith thickness or grain size.
- Earth SoilGrids texture/density → Moon/Mars properties.
- Thermal inertia → bulk density without a verified model and stated assumptions.
- `1 − bulk_density / 2650` → porosity without a stated particle density.
- Penetration resistance → bearing strength without a stated failure criterion
  and reduction model.
- Literature grain-size scalar → particle-size distribution without bin
  fractions, basis and depth.
- Rover wheel slip/trafficability → bearing strength.

The release contains 12 observations: 6 detected (4 Moon, 2 Mars) and 6 explicit
`no_coverage`. Every detected value has explicit depth support, a source locator,
an evidence class and published uncertainty where available. Every no-coverage
row has a blocker and no value.

## 7. Blockers

- Moon PSD: no machine-readable bin/fraction table with basis and depth.
- Moon bearing strength: only cone penetration resistance; no conversion.
- Mars PSD: no measured site-specific distribution; scalar not converted.
- Mars porosity: no direct porosity; no density→porosity conversion.
- Mars friction angle: only an assumed 30° model input.
- Mars bearing strength: only mole penetration resistance.
- Regolith thickness: feature not registered in D06.
- Soil chemistry: no D06 chemistry feature; APXS is audit-only.

This release is bounded evidence, not a global or exhaustive D06 survey. It does
not modify shared scoring, API schemas, the D06 coverage matrix or existing D06
pilot bundles.
