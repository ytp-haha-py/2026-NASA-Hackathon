## D05 — Solar illumination geometry (real, model-derived) and ionizing radiation (separated)

- **`solar_illumination` (D05):** SPICE solar geometry only (model-derived). **Not** observed irradiance/radiance, **not** shadow fraction/horizon visibility, **not** ionizing radiation.
- **`ionizing_radiation` (D05):** real-source audit for absorbed/ionizing dose evidence. **No Gy/Sv fabrication and no flux↔dose conversion without physics.**

### D05 ionizing radiation — real-source search and explicit `no_coverage`

**Audit basis (official sources / repo evidence):** repo D05 coverage/source audits show no committed ionizing dose observations; CRaTER reachable as calibrated orbital data but not direct AOI surface dose (`docs/D05_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`, `docs/data-sources/d05-illumination-radiation-coverage-matrix.json`, `docs/D03_D05_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`). Official products reviewed for scope:
- **Moon:** LRO CRaTER `LRO-L-CRAT-3-CDR-CALIBRATED-V1.0` (DOI 10.17189/1519728) and `LRO-L-CRAT-3/4-DDR-PROCESSED-V1.0` (DOI 10.17189/1519729), NASA PDS — orbital calibrated energy/LET-derived environment, **not** an in-situ or validated surface absorbed/equivalent dose at Shackleton rim. Converting to AOI surface Gy/Sv requires spectral unfolding, detector response, geometry/albedo/secondary particles, shielding and transport/physics assumptions → **not applied; no fabrication**.
- **Mars (Jezero AOI):** no in-situ ionizing particle dosimeter at Jezero. Perseverance **MEDA RDS** measures UV/solar/dust radiative environment, **not** ionizing particle absorbed dose (Gy). MSL **RAD** `MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0` (DOI 10.17189/1519761, PDS) provides real in-situ surface dose-rate evidence at **Gale Crater** (Curiosity) but is **not transferable** to Jezero without uncommitted transport/model assumptions → **no fabricated/extrapolated value**.

**Result (both AOIs):** `feature_id=absorbed_dose_gy` recorded as **`state=no_coverage`**, `value=null`, `score_role=evidence_only`, `subgroup=ionizing_radiation`, `method_version=ionizing-radiation-source-audit-0.1.0`, with explicit spatial/temporal/depth support, uncertainty unknown, QA flags (`separated_from_solar_illumination`, `no_physics_based_dose_conversion`, `no_fabricated_gy_sv`, etc.), and audited source asset IDs/URLs. Illumination and radiation records are separate observations.

## D06 — Moon/Mars regolith & surface properties (official-source audit, no proxies)

**Semantics/constraints:** per `contracts/d06-regolith-semantics-v1.json` and `docs/D06_REGOLITH_SEMANTICS.md` — model prediction ≠ measurement; depth/support/conditions required; detected D06 values need explicit depth/support and defensible provenance; **no DEM roughness/slope, no land-cover proxy, no thermal-inertia shortcut without stated verified physics, no Earth→Moon/Mars transfer, no fabricated values**. Missing/non-encodable values are explicit `no_coverage`/`unknown` (never zero-filled).

**Official sources audited (PDS/literature, bounded):**
- Moon context: NASA PDS Apollo 15 LSRP `urn:nasa:pds:a15lsrp_soil_mechanics` (https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lunar/urn-nasa-pds-a15lsrp_soil_mechanics/), Apollo 16 LSRP `urn:nasa:pds:a16lsrp_soil_mechanics`, Apollo 15/17 HFE `urn:nasa:pds:a15_17_hfe_concatenated`. Apollo PSR/sample literature provides site/sample-specific ranges or model-interpreted mechanics (and some sample densities at other stations) but not direct, defensibly encodable detected D06 records for the **Shackleton rim AOI** with required footprint/depth/support.
- Mars context: NASA PDS InSight HP3 TEM `urn:nasa:pds:insight_hp3_tem`, InSight RAD `urn:nasa:pds:insight_rad` (https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/insight/hp3rad.htm). Spohn et al. 2022 (doi:10.1007/s11214-022-00941-z) reports model-derived/interpretive properties at the **InSight** site (not direct measurements, not the **Jezero** AOI) — not transferable, not ingested as detected "真實值".

**Result (both AOIs, all 6 supported features):** `particle_size_distribution`, `bulk_density_kg_m3`, `porosity_fraction`, `cohesion_kpa`, `friction_angle_deg`, `bearing_strength_kpa` recorded as **`state=no_coverage`**, `value=null`, `score_role=evidence_only`, `method_version=d06-regolith-evidence-audit-0.1.0`, with explicit spatial support (AOI centre point/bounded context, planetary CRS), `temporal_support=unknown`, `depth_support=unknown` and notes stating no verified measurement depth/support for the AOI (depth would be required for any detected D06 value), uncertainty unknown, QA flags prohibiting DEM/slope/landcover/transfer inference, and audited source assets.
