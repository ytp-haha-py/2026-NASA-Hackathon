# D03 thermal + D04 water/ice AOI evidence (Issue #170 Goal E)

Status: **bounded, evidence-only AOI evidence; no D03/D04 score and no global coverage claim.**
This slice adds new official-source D03/D04 evidence for the 23 committed Moon/Mars reference
windows. It does not rewrite the #43/#44 pilots, does not change shared API/schema/scoring, and
does not turn a proxy or a model output into an observed ice detection.

New artifacts: [`pipelines/d02_d04_aoi/thermal_water/`](../pipelines/d02_d04_aoi/thermal_water/).
Builder: `pipelines/d02_d04_aoi/thermal_water/build.py`.
Pure functions: `pipelines/src/analog_pipelines/aoi_thermal_water.py`.
Tests: `pipelines/d02_d04_aoi/tests/test_thermal_water_aoi.py`.

Existing committed evidence is reused and linked, never copied into a new “measured” claim:

- Moon D03: Diviner PCP 240 m bolometric brightness temperature at 00:00-00:15 and 12:00-12:15.
- Mars D03: USGS THEMIS quantitative apparent thermal inertia at 100 m.
- Moon D04: Lunar Prospector NS water-equivalent hydrogen, Mini-RF CPR, and existing
  `water_ice_detection` claims (`unknown`/`no_coverage`/`not_detected` only).
- Mars D04: Odyssey HEND normalized epithermal flux and its `unknown` ice claim.

The GCP, TES and NS requests are bounded HTTP byte ranges (36 GCP rows, 10 TES value/mask
pixels, 5 NS rows). PRP requires the full 604,800,210-byte table because triangle rows are not
indexed by AOI; it is cached under the gitignored `var/cache/d03-d04-aoi-170/` and is not
committed. At most two range workers run concurrently; the single PRP transfer is sequential.
A per-source failure is caught and recorded in `manifest.json.source_status` without stopping
the other sources.

`PRP shallow frac` is the area fraction of selected PRP triangles with modeled stability depth

valid zero, not rewritten as missing.

## Measured / modeled / proxy separation

| Class | Rows in this slice | What they are | What they are not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measured radiometric | Diviner PCP, Diviner GCP | brightness temperature under the product's retrieval/emissivity assumptions | kinetic surface temperature; not a cross-body temperature |
| Derived retrieval | THEMIS TI, TES TIMAP | apparent thermal inertia from different instruments, models and supports | the same quantity/method; TES 2.96 km is never mixed with THEMIS 100 m |
| Measured proxy | Lunar Prospector WEH, Mini-RF CPR, HEND flux, Odyssey NS counts/s | hydrogen/radar/neutron evidence at native support | ice fraction, ice phase, mineable ice, or a detection |
| Modeled | Diviner PRP temperatures and ice-stability depth | annual model outputs from Diviner climatology + Kaguya DEM | observed temperature time series or observed ice |
| Detection claim | existing `water_ice_detection` | `unknown`/`no_coverage`/`not_detected` states with reasons | confirmed ice; **0 confirmed ice detections** |

No D03/D04 feature is a similarity feature in `scoring.py`. No cross-body D03/D04 score is
produced. The values are evidence-only or context-only.

## Why the new sources are not interchangeable

- **GCP vs PCP:** GCP is a 0.5° all-nadir cumulative product (2009-2015), while PCP is a 240 m
  southern-summer composite. Their night values differ substantially (for example,
  `moon-shackleton-rim-site04-roi1`: GCP 67.89 K at 00:00 versus PCP 136.03 K). This is a
  season/support/method difference, not a measurement error, and the two are separate method
  versions. No diurnal range is computed from either pair.
- **TES vs THEMIS:** TES TIMAP is a 2.96 km day/night retrieval from three Mars years; THEMIS is
  a 100 m quantitative mosaic from a different retrieval. Their values are not merged and no
  conversion is applied.
- **PRP vs observed ice:** `ice_depth` is the modeled depth at which water ice would sublimate
  at 1 kg m⁻² per billion years. `0` means stable at the surface; `-999` means the modeled depth
  exceeds 2.8738 m and is excluded. It is not an observed ice table, ice fraction or reserve.
- **Odyssey NS vs HEND:** Odyssey NS stores counts/second on a 0.5° grid; HEND stores a
  normalized epithermal flux ratio on a 4° grid. Different instruments and normalization; the
  NS row carries `not_comparable_to` and is never substituted for HEND.

## Blockers and non-claims

- Only the 23 committed Moon/Mars reference windows are covered. This is **not** a global,
  exhaustive or representative survey.
- No new Moon/Mars AOIs were introduced in this slice; the existing #132/#155 reference set is
  used unchanged.
- GCP, TES and Odyssey NS are coarser than the 1.98 km windows. They are context/proxy evidence,
  not AOI-scale measurements.
- PRP is a model product and has no per-triangle uncertainty. The 0.5 valid-area threshold is a
  presentation rule, not a product uncertainty statement.
- PDS Geosciences directory/catalog pages intermittently returned the “webpage unavailable”
  HTML during this work; every direct product and label URL used here was verified separately.
  The checker records the exact ranges and hashes in `assets.json`.
- No paid service, credential, global raster download, or large binary is committed. The PRP
  source table remains in the gitignored cache.
- No shared API/schema/OpenAPI/scoring/coverage-matrix files were changed.
